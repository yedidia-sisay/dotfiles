# Codex session archives

For each conversation in this repository, finish with a concise, self-contained report in the final assistant message. Cover the whole conversation: goal, important decisions, work, verification, and remaining follow-up. The user-wide Codex `SessionEnd` hook has independent transcript-save and summary-report actions that write under `.cherry/chats/` and `.cherry/sessions/`.

Keep the report useful to a person returning later. Do not include secrets or unrelated private information in it.
