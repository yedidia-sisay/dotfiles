# Codex session archives

For each conversation in this repository, finish with a concise, self-contained report in the final assistant message. Cover the goal, important decisions, files changed, and verification or remaining follow-up. A project-local Codex Stop hook archives the full conversation transcript and saves that final message as the matching report under `.cherry/sessions/` and `.cherry/chats/`.

Keep the report useful to a person returning later. Do not include secrets or unrelated private information in it.
